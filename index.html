Formula: patients times the patient value basis, times the collection rate, times 12. Patient value is billed allowable, not cash; the collection rate converts it. Projections are estimates based on stated assumptions and do not guarantee results.
Methodology. Source: live pull from the Google Sheet "All Raw Data_Confirm" (confirmed or billed orders across all item groups; refreshed nightly at 9pm HST). Orders are unique SO numbers; patients are unique patient names per month; patient months are the sum of monthly unique patients. The real collection rate is measured only on mature months, those with at least 90 days of collection runway. Write off fields are unpopulated in Brightree exports, so open balance is not computed here; collections are cash to date. The current calendar month is partial. Open receivables and AR aging are not part of this export and live in the separate AR workbook. This dashboard re-fetches the sheet on every load, so numbers move with the nightly refresh.
